# Thesis: PUBLIC EXPENDITURES IN BRAZIL: An analysis of gender and race transversality in the federal budget
## Larissa Rosa - Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais - 2023

This repository contains the script and results from Larissa Rosa's undergraduate thesis for Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (UFMG).

The project investigates whether public spending in Brazil treats gender and race as cross-cutting dimensions in fulfilling the State's redistributive role. Using microdata from the 2017–2018 Brazilian Household Budget Survey (POF/IBGE), the study applies econometric models to access to health and education for minority groups.

The study combines two approaches: 
(i) A simulation that reallocates health and education budgets using inverse-income weights, evaluated through the Gini index and Lorenz curve;
(ii) Logit models of the probability that a household receives non-monetary public education and health resources where Households are compared across income classes, gender and race.

##  Repository Structure
* `/1. Larissa Rosa Script.`: Code used for data reading, variable declaration, and statistical estimates.
* `/results`: Tables, graphs, and spreadsheets generated by the scripts.
* `/NPE-058_0121`: Economic policy note
* `/Monografia_Larissa_Maria_Silva`: Undergraduate thesis

**POF 2017–2018 (Brazillian Household Budget Survey):**

*Reproducibility Note: Since the original microdata exceed GitHub's size limit, please download the datasets from the links [https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1ESkstHIq6g2Ms7vSLdPtIL_q0UxSHZVt?usp=sharing] 
In the “Dados20230713” folder, you will find all the .RDS files needed to reproduce the code. You can also find the original files through the IBGE website [https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/sociais/saude/24786-pof-2017-2018.html?=&t=microdados] .*

Once you have downloaded the data from the drive, simply run the script titled “1. Larissa Rosa Script.” This code processes all the data on households and individuals, producing a single table that serves as the basis for conducting various analyses. 

In the results folder, you will find a table showing the main compositions of Brazilian households by income decile, as well as graphs illustrating:
- GINI coefficient based on per capita disposable income
- Composition of household income
- Average years of schooling by income decile
- And econometric analyses on access to health care and education by minority groups in Brazil 

Also attached are the final thesis and a paper on economic policy based on the research conducted in the thesis.
